Group Project Assessment Rubrics
Total: 100 points | 5 components | 11 criteria
Score each criterion with an integer in the matching band; add the scores within each component.
Peer assessment: average the totals received from all other group members (no self-rating).
Poster, presentation, report and code: the same scores for all group members.

Criteria
Excellent (9–10)
Good (7–8)
Fair (4–6)
Poor (0–3)
Project Understanding 
(10)
Accurately explains data, 
workflow and method 
choices.

(10)

some evidence missing.

Explains findings, 
limitations and their 
implications.
Explains findings; 
limitations lack detail.
Repeats results with little

Misinterprets results or

Assessment Notes
Presentation is assessed holistically using the criteria above. Individual contributions are refle cted through peer assessment.
Provide the full dataset or download instructions. If special circumstances prevent you from providing the full original data, include sample data and explain the reason in 
the README. For a documented sample-data exception, assessment is based on the runnable sample workflo w.
Submissions up to one week late incur a deduction of 20% of the earned mark (fin al mark = earned mark × 0.8).
